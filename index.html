I’m xc11. I make music, work behind the stage, and explore what happens when an idea becomes a shared project.

I started X Series in 2016. Its four chapters — XC-V, XC-X, XC-XV and XC-XVI — connect my interests in Minecraft, music, backstage teamwork, and building something together.
